Mixlings Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Mixlings is a puzzle game made by AA Codes LLC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Mixlings has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send any information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, settings, Mixling Book and Daily streak are saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They're deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The Full Garden purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account; the app only learns whether the purchase is owned.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Mixlings can post your scores to leaderboards and unlock achievements. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive your Game Center information. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings.

Children

Mixlings is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
