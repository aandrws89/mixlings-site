Mixlings Support

A cozy color-mixing puzzle. Mix them. Keep them.

Get in touch

Found a bug, stuck on a level, or have an idea? Email support@aacodes.com. It helps to mention your device and the level you were on.

I bought the Full Garden on another device

Open Settings on the title screen and tap Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the unlock sheet. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, stars and Mixling Book, so keep it installed if you want to pick up where you left off.

Is there anything to pay for?

The first 25 levels are free. One purchase, the Full Garden, unlocks the other 75 levels and the Daily puzzle, and it's yours to keep. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

Privacy

Mixlings collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
